    Help & Tips

    Desktop Controls

    Arrow keys or WASD to steer & accelerate. Space to brake. Gamepad left stick + triggers supported. Esc to pause.

    Mobile Controls

    Touch zones: lower-left steers left, lower-right steers right, bottom-right accelerates, upper-right brakes.

    Gameplay Tips

    Smooth, steady driving keeps the sun high. Harsh braking, swerving or going off-road speeds up the sunset. Long stretches of flow make the sun rise and the music bloom.

    Accessibility

    Visual filters can reduce glow intensity (Midnight Mode) for sensitive eyes. All text scales with viewport.

    Relaxation Mode: The Perfect Line

    Watch the 🎯 meter in the HUD — it fills as you settle into the dead-center of the lane at a steady speed. Once it's full, the sunset freezes completely. Drift off-line, brake hard, or stop and it starts sinking again.
